# Privacy Policy

**Landmark Trading**  
Owned and operated by Weston Mitchell (Landmark-Matrix)

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy explains what information Landmark Trading may collect and how it is used. The app is a private product for personal use.

## Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

- Account details, such as name and email, if you sign in (including through Google)
- Basic usage data needed to run the app, such as login time, feature use, and error logs
- Information you choose to enter in the app, such as watchlists, tickers, or settings
- Technical data such as device type, browser, IP address, and cookies or similar identifiers

We do not sell your personal information.

## How we use it

We use this information to:

- Create and manage your account
- Operate scanners, news trackers, and other app features
- Keep the app secure and debug problems
- Communicate with you about access, outages, or account issues

## Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we receive the information Google shares with the app for authentication, typically your name and email. We use that only to identify you and keep you signed in. We do not use Google data for advertising.

## Sharing

We do not share personal information except:

- With service providers that help run the app (for example hosting, auth, or logging), under an obligation to protect it
- If required by law
- If needed to protect the security of the app or its users

## Retention

We keep account and usage data only as long as needed to operate the app or as required by law. If access is revoked or the app is shut down, data may be deleted.

## Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect information, but no system is perfectly secure. Use the app at your own risk.

## Your choices

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your account data by contacting the email listed on the Landmark-Matrix / Landmark Trading GitHub organization page.

## Children

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. Continued use after an update means you accept the new policy.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact email listed on the Landmark-Matrix / Landmark Trading GitHub organization page.
